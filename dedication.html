Dedication

This Website is Dedicated to My Parents

This platform, which brings together the teachings of Sahih Muslim, Islamic articles, and modern technology to make knowledge more accessible, is humbly dedicated to my parents.

They Taught Me How to Live

They did not just give me life. They taught me how to live, instilled in me the love for faith, and showed me the value of knowledge.

For My Father

Dear Father, your hard work, your prayers, and your guidance have been the foundation of my life.

If I am able to do anything meaningful today, it is because of your upbringing.

I pray that Allah accepts this effort as a Sadaqah Jariyah for you, and grants you reward for every person who benefits from it.

For My Mother

Dear Mother, your prayers have been with me at every step of my journey.

Your love and patience have been my greatest strength. If there is any الخير in this platform, it is a reflection of your duas.

May Allah grant you health, peace, and a long blessed life, and elevate your مقام in Jannah.

To My Family

I am deeply grateful to my wife and children.

They gave me time, supported me through my commitments, and allowed me the space to focus on building this platform.

This is not just my work. It is a shared sacrifice of our family.

My Purpose

The purpose of this website is simple: to make the teachings of Hadith accessible to everyone.

If even one person comes closer to faith through this platform, I pray that the reward is granted to my parents.

Final Words

Some efforts are for this world, and some are for the hereafter.

This effort is for my parents.
